Privacy Policy — Rolling Punk Ball

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nika Studios handles information when you use Rolling Punk Ball, what information is involved, how it is used and shared, and the choices and rights you have.

By installing or playing the Game you accept the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with them, please do not use the Game.

Interpretation and Definitions

Interpretation

Words that begin with a capital letter have the meanings defined below. These definitions apply whether the words appear in the singular or the plural.

Definitions

In this Privacy Policy:

  • Application or Game means Rolling Punk Ball, the software provided by the Company that You download and install on a Device.
  • Company (also “We”, “Us” or “Our”) means Nika Studios, the developer of the Game.
  • Device means any device that can run the Game, such as a phone or tablet.
  • Service Provider means a third-party company that processes information on behalf of the Company, such as the advertising mediation platform and the ad networks described below.
  • Personal Data means any information that relates to an identified or identifiable person.
  • Service means the Game.
  • Usage Data means information collected automatically while the Game is used, such as device type, operating system and the time and duration of use.
  • You means the person using the Game.

Collecting and Using Your Information

Information You Provide

The Game has no accounts, no sign-in and no registration. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details, and the Game does not access your contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.

Information Stored on Your Device

Your game progress — unlocked levels, coins, gems, items bought in the in-game shop with in-game currency, and settings such as sound volume, graphics quality and control type — is saved only on your Device. It is not sent to Us, and it is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

Usage Data Collected by Advertising Partners

The Game is free and is supported by advertising. Ads are delivered by Yodo1 MAS, an ad mediation platform that works with third-party ad networks such as Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads, ironSource, Liftoff (Vungle), Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Pangle, InMobi, Fyber, BidMachine, Bigo, Moloco and Yandex. When the Game requests and shows an ad, these Service Providers may automatically collect Usage Data such as:

  • your Device’s advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • device information such as model, manufacturer, operating system version, language, screen size and mobile carrier;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country or city level);
  • information about ads you see and interact with (views, clicks, installs);
  • diagnostic information such as crashes and performance data of the ad software.

We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you personally.

How the Information Is Used

  • To run the Game and remember your progress and settings on your Device.
  • To show ads that keep the Game free, including personalised ads where You have allowed them.
  • To measure ad performance, limit how often the same ad is shown, and prevent fraud.
  • To find and fix technical problems with the ad software.

Sharing of Information

The information described above is collected and used by the Service Providers that deliver ads. We do not sell your Personal Data. Information may also be disclosed:

  • For business transfers — if the Company is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this Privacy Policy will continue to apply or You will be notified of any change;
  • With Your consent — for any other purpose You agree to.

Each Service Provider handles data under its own privacy policy, for example the Yodo1 Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. The full list of ad networks and links to their policies is provided by Yodo1.

Your Choices and Consent

  • If You are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for Your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change Your choice at any time from the consent prompt.
  • On Android You can reset Your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on Your device). You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.
  • You can stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the Game.

Retention of Information

Game progress stays on Your Device until You uninstall the Game or clear its data. Information collected by Service Providers is kept by them only for as long as their own policies and the law require.

Transfer of Information

Service Providers may process information on servers located outside Your country, where data-protection laws can differ from those where You live. They are required to protect that information with appropriate safeguards.

Disclosure of Information

The Company may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities, or in the good-faith belief that it is necessary to:

  • comply with a legal obligation;
  • protect and defend the rights or property of the Company;
  • prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing in connection with the Game;
  • protect the personal safety of users or the public;
  • protect against legal liability.

Security of Information

We take the security of information seriously and rely on Service Providers that use industry-standard protection, including encryption in transit. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Children’s Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from them. If You are a parent or guardian and believe Your child has provided Personal Data, please contact Us and We will take steps to have it removed.

Links to Other Websites

Ads shown in the Game may link to websites or apps that are not operated by Us. If You tap such an ad, You will leave the Game. We have no control over, and are not responsible for, the content or privacy practices of those third parties, so please review their privacy policies.

Your Rights

Depending on where You live, You may have the right to access, correct or delete Personal Data about You, or to object to its processing. Because We do not hold Personal Data about You, most requests can be met by uninstalling the Game or clearing its data. For data held by an ad network, You can contact that Service Provider directly or contact Us and We will help. You can contact Us with any request.

Changes to this Privacy Policy
